# RadNet, Inc.

> RadNet, Inc. (NASDAQ: RDNT) is the largest provider of freestanding, fixed-site outpatient diagnostic imaging services in the United States, operating roughly 440 imaging centers (MRI, CT, PET/CT, ultrasound, mammography, and other modalities). Beyond its clinical network, RadNet runs a health-technology business spanning eRAD (cloud imaging workflow, PACS and RIS) and DeepHealth, its AI and digital-health division whose cloud-native "DeepHealth OS" unifies enterprise imaging with AI-powered population-health screening. RadNet does not publish a public developer API or developer portal; its technology is delivered as enterprise imaging platforms and AI products through eRAD and DeepHealth.

## Technology portfolio

- eRAD: web-based, cloud imaging workflow, PACS and RIS solutions.
- DeepHealth OS: cloud-native enterprise imaging (Diagnostic Suite, Operations Suite, TechLive) with AI-powered population-health screening for breast, lung, prostate, neuro, and thyroid imaging.
